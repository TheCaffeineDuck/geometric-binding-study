dN/N values: t0 = imposed (instantaneous N at the disturbance phase); settled = period-averaged N of the disturbed run over the control's, window t_d+750..2000. Rates are -d ln E(r<3hw)/dt (log fit of E3, t_d+500..2000; late = mean of the 500-wide windows after t_d+2000 while frac > 0.5). A break is `instantaneous` if E(r<3hw) < 0.5 E_before within 7 time units of the kick, else `dynamic`.
